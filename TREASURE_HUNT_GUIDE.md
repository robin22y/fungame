# Treasure Hunt / Scavenger Hunt Guide

## How to Create a Multi-Step Treasure Hunt

### Example: Kitchen Scavenger Hunt (£1 reward)

#### Step 1: Create Your QR Tags

Go to **Add Tag** and create tags for each location:

**Tag 1 - Under Stairs**
- Name: `Under Stairs`
- Tag ID: `STAIRS001` (or click "Simulate NFC Tap")
- Message: `Great job finding me! Take the pen from here and go to the kitchen. Scan the QR code near the microwave for your next clue!`
- Click "Generate QR Code"
- Print the QR code

**Tag 2 - Kitchen Microwave**
- Name: `Kitchen Microwave`
- Tag ID: `KITCHEN001`
- Message: `You found the kitchen! Look inside the microwave - there's a delicious cake waiting for you! You've completed the treasure hunt! 🎉`
- Click "Generate QR Code"
- Print the QR code

#### Step 2: View & Print All Tags

- Go to **View Tags** tab
- Click "Print All" to print all QR codes at once
- Cut out the QR codes
- Place them at their locations:
  - Tape "Under Stairs" QR code under the stairs
  - Tape "Kitchen Microwave" QR code near the microwave

#### Step 3: Create the Multi-Checkpoint Mission

Go to **Add Mission**:
1. **Enable Multi-Checkpoint Quest** - Toggle ON the "Multi-Checkpoint Quest" switch
2. Click "Add Checkpoint (Step 1)" and select "Under Stairs"
3. Click "Add Checkpoint (Step 2)" and select "Kitchen Microwave"
4. You'll see the quest path: `Under Stairs → Kitchen Microwave`
5. Fill in mission details:
   - Task Name: `Kitchen Treasure Hunt`
   - Emoji: `🏆` or `🎂`
   - XP Points: `50`
   - Pocket Money: `£1.00`
   - Assign To: Choose your child
6. Click "Create Mission"

#### Step 4: How Kids Play

1. Kid logs in and sees mission: "Kitchen Treasure Hunt" with **Step 1 of 2**
2. Kid goes to stairs and taps "Scan/Tap Tag"
3. Scans QR code under stairs
4. Sees "✅ Checkpoint Reached! Step 1 of 2 ✓"
5. Sees message: "Great job finding me! Take the pen..."
6. Sees "Keep going! 1 more step to go!"
7. Kid follows instructions to kitchen
8. Scans QR code near microwave
9. Sees "🎉 Quest Complete! Step 2 of 2 ✓"
10. Sees message: "You found the kitchen! Look inside..."
11. Gets confetti celebration + £1 + 50 XP!

## More Treasure Hunt Ideas

### 🧹 **Chore Quest**
Create tags in each room with cleaning instructions:
1. Bedroom - "Pick up toys"
2. Bathroom - "Put towels away"
3. Kitchen - "Load dishwasher"
4. Living Room - "Vacuum floor - Congratulations!"

### 🎒 **Morning Routine Hunt**
Help kids remember their morning tasks:
1. Bathroom - "Brush teeth ✅"
2. Bedroom - "Make bed ✅"
3. Kitchen - "Eat breakfast ✅"
4. Front Door - "Grab backpack - Ready for school!"

### 🎁 **Birthday Surprise Hunt**
Lead them to presents or party locations:
1. Living Room - "Check under the sofa!"
2. Garden - "Look near the big tree!"
3. Garage - "Your surprise is in the red box!"

### 📚 **Educational Hunt**
Turn learning into a game:
1. Math Problem - "Solve: 5+7=? Then go to room number 12"
2. Reading Clue - "Find a book with a red cover"
3. Science - "Where do we keep frozen things?" → Freezer
4. Final Prize - "You're a genius! Here's your reward"

## Tips for Great Treasure Hunts

1. **Start Simple** - For young kids, use 2-3 locations max
2. **Use Photos** - Add picture clues in the message field
3. **Time Challenges** - Use Poker Offers for speed bonuses
4. **Team Hunts** - Assign same mission to multiple kids
5. **Weekly Themes** - Pirate Monday, Detective Tuesday, etc.
6. **Reuse Tags** - Same QR codes can be used for different missions
7. **Print Backup** - Keep extra copies of popular location tags

## Advanced: Chain Missions

Create multiple missions that unlock sequentially:

**Mission 1:** "Find the Red Tag" → Reward: 10 XP
**Mission 2:** "Find the Blue Tag" → Reward: 20 XP
**Mission 3:** "Find the Gold Tag" → Reward: 50 XP + £2

## No QR Codes? No Problem!

You can also create simple missions without any tags:
- Task Name: "Clean Your Room"
- Leave Tag dropdown as "No tag - Simple task"
- Kid just taps "Mark Complete" when done
- Still gets XP + money + confetti!
